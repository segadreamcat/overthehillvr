over the hill vr

Get in, grab the wheel, and go get lost in the mountains. No wheel? An Xbox controller or Quest controllers work too.

This is the full mod, with MelonLoader 0.7.3, the cockpit, mirrors, dashboard, console GPS, automatic rain wipers, and controller bindings included. You still need your own Windows Steam copy of Over the Hill. This is PCVR, so your Quest needs to be connected to your PC.

Install

1. Close the game. If you already have mods, back up your Mods, MelonLoader, and UserData folders and version.dll first.
2. Install SteamVR and the .NET 6 Desktop Runtime, Windows x64: https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet/6.0. For a G29, install Logitech G HUB too.
3. In Steam, right-click Over the Hill > Manage > Browse local files. Extract everything from this ZIP right there, beside over the hill.exe. Merge folders and replace files. Don't put it inside another folder.
4. In Steam > Properties > Launch Options, add -force-d3d11.
5. Connect your headset to your PC, open SteamVR, then launch the game through Steam. The first launch needs internet and can take a few minutes while the loader sets things up. Let it finish.

Zoom all the way in to get into the driver's seat. The custom cockpit and dashboard are used across cars and paint jobs. The center-console GPS shows the local map and your heading. It follows you automatically; no extra buttons needed. When it rains, droplets collect on the windshield and the wipers start automatically.

Gamepad

Xbox button names here. Use the matching buttons on your pad.

- Left stick: steer. RT: gas. LT: brake.
- LB / RB: gear down / up. Check the dash for your gear.
- D-pad right: parking brake. D-pad left: headlights.
- D-pad up / down: change view and zoom. In the cockpit, tap up to toggle the roof; hold up to change interior style.
- X: 4H / 4L. Y: attach winch. A: interact, extend winch, or confirm. B: release winch or go back.
- Left stick click: horn. Menu: pause.
- Click both sticks: recenter. Hold both for a screenshot.
- View + Y: VR on / off. View + B: HUD on / off. View + D-pad: adjust your seat.

Quest controllers

Turn off your Xbox controller and unplug the wheel first. If either is connected, it keeps control. Wake both Quest controllers before launching.

- Hold either grip, or both, with your hands like you're holding a steering wheel. Turn your hands to steer. Let go to return to center.
- Right trigger: gas. Left trigger: brake. Left stick also steers when you're not gripping.
- Right stick left / right: gear down / up. Let it return to center between shifts.
- Right stick up / down: change view and zoom.
- Click and release the right stick: parking brake. Click both sticks together: recenter.
- X: 4H / 4L. Y: attach winch. A: interact, extend winch, or confirm. B: release winch or go back.
- Left stick: navigate menus. Hold the left stick click for one second to pause. Select Main Menu with the stick and A to leave the drive.

Wheel

Tested with the Logitech G29. Leave G HUB running, set its operating range to 900 degrees, and disable Steam Input for the wheel. Keep keyboard and mouse assignments out of the G HUB desktop and game profiles.

- Wheel: steer. Pedals: gas and brake. Force feedback is included.
- Right paddle: gear up. Left paddle: gear down. The dash shows the selected gear.
- D-pad right: parking brake. Left: headlights. Up / down: view and zoom.
- Cross: interact / confirm. Circle: release winch / back. Square: 4H / 4L. Triangle: attach winch.
- Options: pause. Plus: recenter. Minus: screenshot.
- Red dial: seat up / down. Press the dial: HUD on / off. Hold PS for a second: VR on / off.

If something gets weird

F8 toggles VR, F6 recenters, and F11 switches to the cockpit. Page Up / Down moves your seat up / down; Home / End moves it forward / back; Insert / Delete moves it sideways. F12 toggles the HUD.

Launching flat? Check that SteamVR sees your headset and that -force-d3d11 is set, then try F8. Loader won't start? Check .NET 6 x64 and the Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist.

Settings live in UserData/MelonPreferences.cfg. If something breaks, send me MelonLoader/Latest.log and tell me which controller you used.

To uninstall, close the game and restore your backup. On a clean install, removing this ZIP's version.dll stops the loader and mod from loading.
